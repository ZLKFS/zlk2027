Ķūķis – Latvian barley stew

Recipe: Māra Krēsliņa, Stockholm · ≈ 3 h 30 min

Māra Krēsliņa's ķūķis is based on H. Birziņa's cookbook of 1936, which Māra's sister Zuze once copied in the library of the Latvian Gymnasium in Münster. Māra has adapted the recipe and made a vegetarian version too. Māra's ķūķis is famous – in an episode of the programme “Globālais latvietis”, folklorist Austris Grasis admits he received the recipe for his excellent ķūķis by text message from Māra Krēsliņa, on the ferry from Stockholm to Riga. He has always made ķūķis on New Year's Eve – four hours in the oven, and it is ready around midnight!

Ingredients for about 6 servings

Method

  1. 1Toast the barley in a dry pan until well browned.
  2. 2Finely chop and fry the onions and pork.
  3. 3Layer in a pot or casserole: first the toasted barley, then the fried pork with onions, then barley again and so on, until the pot is full.
  4. 4Pour over the stock.
  5. 5Cook in the oven for about 3 hours at 200 °C; do not stir, but top up with liquid if needed.

Māra's advice: “When I can, I buy the pork in Latvia, because Swedish pork is nowhere near as good. No need to stick slavishly to the proportions, it turns out well either way.”
