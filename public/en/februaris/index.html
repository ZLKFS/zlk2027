Latvian School leek soup

Recipe: Valdis Dzelzkalējs, Stockholm · ≈ 40 min

Valdis Dzelzkalējs, a Stockholmer from Ķekava, adapted this recipe from the recipe collection of the Swedish dairy cooperative Arla for the Latvian School in Stockholm – to the school's needs and taste. He says: “Everyone usually eats the leek soup – even those who are particular about food.”

Method

  1. 1Peel and chop the potatoes and leeks.
  2. 2Melt the butter in a large pot and sauté the leeks.
  3. 3Add the potatoes and stock and cook for about 10 minutes, until the potatoes are soft.
  4. 4Blend everything into a smooth soup with a hand blender.
  5. 5Add the seasoning and the cream.
  6. 6Bring to the boil.

Done!
