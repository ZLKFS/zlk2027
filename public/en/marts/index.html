Easter paska – Birziņa's curd cheese dessert, Māra's way

Recipe: Māra Krēsliņa, Stockholm · ≈ 1 h

Māra Krēsliņa is a Stockholm Latvian who grew up in New York. Her recipe comes from H. Birziņa's cookbook of 1936, which Māra's sister Zuze once copied in the library of the Latvian Gymnasium in Münster. Māra has added to it and adapted it to Swedish conditions. In her family the dish has always been called paska. Work starts on Maundy Thursday!

Ingredients for about 8 servings

Method

  1. Start on Maundy Thursday
  2. 1Heat the 2 litres of soured milk until curds form. Pour off the whey.
  3. 2Beat the butter, sugar and flavourings and add them to the curds.
  4. 3Stir in the eggs one at a time.
  5. 4Stir in the coarsely chopped nuts and the grated lemon zest.
  6. 5Whip the cream and fold it into the paska mixture.
  7. 6Wrap the mixture in cheesecloth, put it in a paska mould and place it under a weight, much like Jāņi cheese. Keep it in the fridge until Easter Sunday.
  8. 7Decorate with raisins, pieces of dried fruit and chopped nuts if you like. Serve as a dessert with berry sauce.

Māra has a special wooden paska mould held together with wooden pegs. If you have none, a clean, unused flowerpot will do!
