Benita's crispy pork chop with potatoes and sauerkraut

Recipe: Laila Strunke, Stockholm · ≈ 40 min

Laila Strunke from Stockholm remembers this and other dishes her mother Benita cooked: “Mum's food was delicious, but she never used recipes. She cooked from memory and by taste.” Benita grew up in Ventspils; her parents came from Alsunga and Jūrkalne. Perhaps the word “kraupštīgs” – crispy – comes from Courland?

Method

  1. 1Beat the chops out well on both sides and season with a little salt and black pepper.
  2. 2Coat each slice in flour, then beaten egg and finally breadcrumbs.
  3. 3Melt butter in a frying pan, add a little oil and heat. Fry the chops in the hot pan on both sides until golden brown and the coating is crisp.

Serve with boiled floury potatoes or the season's new potatoes. Depending on the time of year, choose braised sauerkraut with a little sugar, or a cold cabbage salad made by mixing fresh cabbage and sauerkraut with soured cream, caraway, salt, black pepper and a pinch of sugar.

Enjoy your meal!
