Tiramisu from Bērzgale

Recipe: Sofija Ieva Otisone, Sigtuna · ≈ 45 min

The recipe was sent in by Sofija Ieva Otisone from Sigtuna. Her mother comes from Courland and her father from Vidzeme, but she secretly tasted tiramisu in Bērzgale, in Rēzekne municipality, when she was very small. Ever since, tiramisu has been Sofija's favourite dish. She makes it with her mum, and tiramisu reminds Sofija of Latvia.

Method

  1. 1Heat water in a pot and set a bowl with the egg yolks and 50 g of sugar over it. Beat the mixture with an electric whisk until the sugar has dissolved. When the yolks have turned paler, add 1 tsp of vanilla sugar.
  2. 2Take the bowl off the heat and whisk in the mascarpone.
  3. 3In a separate bowl, whip the cream with the sugar until stiff.
  4. 4Fold the whipped cream into the mascarpone mixture.
  5. 5Prepare the blackcurrant juice and add 1 tsp of vanilla sugar.
  6. 6Take a nice glass dish with high sides.
  7. 7Dip each biscuit on one side in the juice and lay them in rows in the dish. When the whole base is covered with biscuits, spread the cream over them. Sift cocoa and chocolate over the white cream.
  8. 8Add another layer of ladyfingers and continue until you have three layers. The last is a thick layer of cream, dusted with cocoa and chocolate.
  9. 9Put the dish in the fridge for about 8 hours.
  10. 10Enjoy!

Remember: the dessert tastes best if it rests in the fridge for at least 8 hours before eating.
