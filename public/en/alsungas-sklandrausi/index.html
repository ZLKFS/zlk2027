Alsunga sklandrauši

Recipe: Agnese Blaubārde, Stockholm · ≈ 2 h

Designer Agnese Blaubārde comes from Pāvilosta but calls Stockholm and the Latvian School in Stockholm home.

Agnese tells what sklandrausis means to her: “I chose this recipe as a Courlander out in the world. Alsunga sklandrausis were always baked by my grandmother Ruta. For me they belong with her and with home. That taste I want to keep and pass on.”

Ingredients for about 20 sklandrauši

  • Dough
  • Potato filling
  • Carrot filling
  • Topping

Method

  1. 1Make the dough. Mix the flour, salt and lard or butter. Add the water little by little and knead until the dough is firm and dense. Do not pour in all the water at once – it may be too much.
  2. 2Shape the bases. On a floured surface, roll the dough into a log and cut it into slices about 2 cm thick. Roll each slice into a thinner round. Turn the edges up with your fingers to make a little basket. Place them on a baking tray.
  3. 3Mix the potato filling. Add the curd cheese, soured cream, butter, eggs, salt and caraway to the potatoes. Mix until smooth.
  4. 4Mix the carrot filling. Add the butter, cream, eggs and sugar to the carrots. Mix to a thick purée.
  5. 5Fill the sklandrauši. Spread potato filling in each basket first and smooth it. Pile plenty of carrot filling on top. Sprinkle with caraway.
  6. 6Bake. Put them in an oven heated to 200 °C and bake for about 40 minutes.
  7. 7Add the topping (if you like). Put a tablespoon of soured cream on each baked sklandrausis, smooth it and sprinkle with sugar.

Agnese's tip: Eat them warm or cooled – whichever you like best!
