Kesella quark cake

Recipe: Kristīne Dannenberga, Stockholm · ≈ 1 h 40 min

This recipe was sent in by Kristīne Dannenberga from Stockholm. Here is its story: “Swedish buns have always seemed too sweet and too rich to me, so I was very glad when I once came across a good basic recipe for a cake whose main ingredient is the fresh cheese Kesella, that is, quark. I had also tasted a similar cake at a lovely bakery at Saltå kvarn. I have changed the recipe a little so the taste comes closer to the Latvian biezpienmaize, curd cheese cake. All the ingredients can be found in any grocery shop.”

Method

  1. 1In a food processor with the blade, pulse the flour with the butter and sugar until crumbly. Add the egg and keep mixing.
  2. 2Press the pastry into a 24 cm springform tin, up the sides as well. Put the tin in the freezer while you make the filling.
  3. 3For the filling, separate the eggs. Whisk the whites until stiff.
  4. 4Put the Kesella, egg yolks, finely chopped apricots, raisins, lemon zest, the sugar mixed with the vanilla sugar, semolina and cornflour in a bowl. Mix thoroughly with an electric whisk. Then carefully fold in the whisked egg whites.
  5. 5Pour the filling into the pastry case and cover with aluminium foil.
  6. 6Bake in the lower part of an oven heated to 200 °C for 45 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for about 20 minutes more, until the cake is a lovely golden colour.
  7. 7Let the cake cool in the tin. Dust with icing sugar before serving. Serve with warm berry sauce.
