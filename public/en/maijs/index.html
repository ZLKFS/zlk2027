Vineta's Persian and Jewish salads – from Ainaži to Sollentuna

Recipe: Vineta Kalniņa-Soleimani, Sollentuna · ≈ 20 min

May – the month of salads! One of our recipes has Eastern European Jewish roots, the other comes from Persian cooking. For the salad recipes we thank Vineta Kalniņa-Soleimani. She comes from Ainaži and has lived for many years in Sollentuna municipality, just outside Stockholm.

Both salads – and plenty of other good food – come out of Vineta's kitchen!
