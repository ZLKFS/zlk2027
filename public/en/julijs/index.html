Skrubba's sentimental Latvian leaf salad

Recipe: Laine Lasmane Wikmark, Täby · ≈ 10 min

Laine Lasmane Wikmark lives in Täby, but her family's roots are in Apriķi parish in Courland, in the countryside around Sigulda and in Ukraine. The recipe Laine sent in is very short and easy to make, but it has a long and beautiful story: “In the 1960s my sisters and I spent our summers at the Latvian children's camp at Skrubba, just south of Stockholm. We were a big crowd of children – around a hundred, as I remember it. The kitchen was ruled by Mrs Šmitmanis and Mrs Ķesteris, together with young helpers. We girls were especially fond of the helpers Vija, Dzintra and Astra. We could pour our hearts out to them when we needed to. Almost every day this very simple and tasty salad was on the table as a side dish. Time passed, and in the 1970s I was a helper myself at a Latvian children's camp – at Ladvik near Vaxholm. The ‘queen’ of the kitchen was Mrs Sausiņš, and I learned to make the salad. Much later I made this simple salad for my own family and named it the ‘sentimental salad’. But Skrubba's dear helpers still call it ‘the Latvian salad’!”
